# US_Sales_Looker_Dashboard
Looker Dashboard Google Data Analysis

Based on the metrics and visualizations presented in **looker report.pdf**, the primary objective of this dashboard is to **track, analyze, and optimize website performance and e-commerce health**.

Specifically, it serves to answer three main business questions:

* **Who is the audience and where are they located?** By monitoring `geoNetwork.country` and pageviews, the objective is to identify primary geographical markets (such as the United States and India) to guide localized marketing budgets and regional targeting.


* **What technology are users interacting with?** By breaking down total visitors by browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.), the report aims to inform the technical team on which platforms to prioritize for quality assurance, device optimization, and seamless user experience.


* **How are business transactions trending over time?** By plotting daily transactions and record counts, the objective is to monitor operational volume, identify cyclical purchasing patterns, evaluate the success of early-July promotional campaigns, and flag potential technical drops or checkout issues.



Ultimately, the report acts as a centralized tool for stakeholders to make data-driven decisions across marketing, technical development, and sales forecasting.
